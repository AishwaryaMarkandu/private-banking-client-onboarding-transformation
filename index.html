Financial Services Transformation Case Study

Private Banking Client Onboarding Transformation

I designed a synthetic Luxembourg private-banking scenario to demonstrate how I would diagnose operational friction, connect it to client and compliance outcomes, and translate the findings into a target operating model and transformation roadmap.

Executive view

I used a reproducible synthetic dataset of 6,000 onboarding cases. These figures are illustrative and do not represent a real bank.

AS-IS onboarding journey

I modelled the journey around the activities that drive client onboarding effort, risk assessment and approval.

01 · Intake

Client profile and initial data capture.

02 · Evidence

Document collection and completeness.

03 · KYC / AML

Identity, ownership, source of wealth/funds and screening.

04 · Decision

Risk assessment, compliance and acceptance.

05 · Activation

Account opening and handover.

My transformation hypothesis: I would not remove risk controls to accelerate onboarding. I would simplify the path around them, route cases by risk and automate evidence-heavy activities while retaining appropriate human oversight.

Operational diagnosis

I focused on cycle time, SLA performance, manual effort and rework.

Monthly onboarding volume

What I would investigate first

  • Document collection and completeness
  • Beneficial ownership and source-of-wealth evidence
  • Risk-based routing
  • Case visibility for relationship managers
  • Exception and SLA management

Target operating model

I would move from a sequential, hand-off-heavy process towards a risk-based and orchestrated model.

Front office

Single onboarding cockpit, transparent blockers and proactive client communication.

Operations & Compliance

Risk-based routing, standardised evidence and controlled exception handling.

Technology

Workflow orchestration, document validation, screening integration and management information.

Transformation priorities

I prioritised initiatives using business value, feasibility, risk reduction and client experience.

Illustrative business case

I used conservative scenario assumptions. They are not observed bank results.

Transformation roadmap

Regulatory design principles

Risk-based CDD

I designed the target model around proportionate risk-based due diligence.

Human accountability

I keep higher-risk and exception decisions under appropriate human ownership.

Operational resilience

I treat workflow and technology changes as controlled transformation initiatives.

My recommendation: I would start with three moves: establish a single onboarding KPI and exception cockpit, redesign document intake and completeness validation, and introduce risk-based workflow routing. I would then use the measured baseline to build the business case for broader orchestration and automation.
